# SyriaRadio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

SyriaRadio is an independent radio player provided by Mohammed Shieko. This
policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and the
choices available to you.

## Information stored on your device

SyriaRadio does not require an account. Favorites, language, appearance,
streaming quality and playback preferences are stored locally on your device.
The developer does not operate a server that stores your listening history or
favorites. Deleting the app removes this locally stored app data.

## Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners
may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising
interactions and diagnostic data, subject to your consent, region and device
privacy settings. Google describes its practices in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

A verified Premium entitlement prevents SyriaRadio from requesting banner and
audio advertisements. The advertising SDK may still be initialized as part of
the app before the App Store entitlement check completes.

## Radio streams

When you play a station, your device connects directly to the station or its
streaming provider. That third party may receive technical request data such as
your IP address, request time and device networking information. SyriaRadio
does not control the content, logging or retention practices of those providers.

## Purchases, restores and reviews

Apple processes Premium purchases, entitlements, payments, restoration and App
Store reviews. SyriaRadio receives only the verified entitlement required to
unlock Premium and does not receive payment-card details. See
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for Apple's data
practices.

## Permissions and sensitive information

SyriaRadio does not request access to contacts, precise location, photos,
camera or microphone. Audio continues in the background only when you choose
to play a radio stream.

## Retention, choices and deletion

- Local preferences remain until you change them or delete the app.
- Advertising and streaming providers retain data under their own policies.
- You can manage tracking and advertising choices in iOS Settings.
- Deleting SyriaRadio removes the app's locally stored preferences.
- For information voluntarily sent to the developer by email, you can request
  access or deletion using the contact address below.

## Children

SyriaRadio is a general-audience radio app and is not specifically directed at
children under 13. The app does not knowingly ask children to provide personal
information directly to the developer.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when SyriaRadio, applicable law or integrated
third-party services change. The effective date above will be updated when a
new version becomes applicable.
